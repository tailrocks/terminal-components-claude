## Do not confuse three milestones

1. **Documented:** the requirement, owner, and rejection test are specified.
2. **Implemented:** the source or enforcement mechanism exists and has been inspected.
3. **Verified:** the required tests and independent reviews ran on the accepted subject.

This pack supplies the first milestone. It does not claim the second or third.
Do not rename `documented` to `verified` to close the current task.
