## Prepared-buffer allocation regression

The picker-stack builder compared baseline `4a0ab4983b84b4909d0182a323eb6e1714651691` with integrated tip `200cdcb43c411813309df5b1ff204fc346e74ae3`. Its eight new allocation failures all ran through repeated `Harness::draw`; the 100-iteration list/tree tests each recorded exactly 100 allocations, and one-frame paint cases recorded one. The cause was `Runtime::prepare` constructing `Buffer::empty(area)` on every prepared frame (Ratatui `Buffer::filled` creates a fresh `Vec<Cell>`). This is confirmed by the exact builder command after the fix: all 11 targeted regressions passed, including all eight failures. Runtime retains the prepared buffer across successful presentation and invalidation, then `resize`s and `reset`s it before repainting; no widget or test thresholds changed.
