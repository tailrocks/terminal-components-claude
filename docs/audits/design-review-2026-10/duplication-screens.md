# Duplication review: screens

Read-only review of the app screens and pages for hand-built UI that belongs in the library. Recorded 2026-10-16. The consolidated decisions and the execution order are in [`docs/architecture/shared-components-plan.md`](../../architecture/shared-components-plan.md).

This review was read-only. The scratch worktree was based on `origin/design/interaction-polish` at `d5ad6ff`, and I removed it at the end. I measured the app-shell numbers myself; the per-screen numbers come from three per-app audits, and I spot-checked their key citations. Line counts in "Lines saved" are estimates. All paths are under `src/bin/`.

**Lines saved by app (estimates):**
- holla: ~2,650 in screens, plus ~1,000 of shell code.
- jackin_preview: ~3,000 in screens, plus ~1,500 of shell code.
- tablepro: ~1,240, plus ~800 of shell code.

**Total: about 11,500 lines deleted from apps; about 1,000 moved into the library.**

**Bugs found along the way:**
- **Blur drift:** holla doesn't tell its Form and Browser modals that focus left; jackin does.
- **Unstable row ids:** jackin manager.rs:1625 keys rows by screen y, so a row's id changes when the layout changes.
- **Dead code:**
  - `wrapped_arrow` does nothing (jackin cockpit.rs:531-535).
  - `saved_focus` is never used (jackin capsule.rs:1643/1659).
  - `modal_hints` returns an empty string (jackin app.rs:2556).
- **Tab-list mismatch:** in tablepro, filtering the tab list rebuilds the items with a different glyph, detail and tag (app.rs:1655 vs 1682).

## New library components to add

1. **`shell::AppShell<W, S: Screen<W>, M: ModalKind>`**: `AppShell::new(theme).regions(Regions{header,strip,footer}).min_size(w,h).help(HelpSpec)`, then `handle(&mut self, Input, &mut W) -> Outcome` and `render(&mut self, &mut Frame, &W)`. It owns the hits, focus ring, pointer, `ModalStack`, blur fan-out and the outside-click policy. Apps implement `Screen<W> { on_key, on_click, on_press, on_drag, on_wheel, on_blur, on_paste, on_tick, on_modal, render, hints, crumb, status }`, using `Cx<W>`, `Request<G>` and `ModalTag`. Adopted by: all 4 shells, plus every holla and jackin screen.
2. **`ModalKind` trait** on Dialog, Picker, ChoiceDialog, FormDialog, InfoDialog, HelpOverlay, ContextMenu and FileBrowser: `on_key`, `on_click`, `on_blur`, `hints()`, `cancel_result()`, `dismiss()`. This removes the per-app `Modal` and `ModalResult` enums and the per-type outside-click mapping.
3. **`FormPanel`**: `FormPanel::new(id, fields).tabs([...]).columns(2).actions([...])`; `render(area,buf,ctx)`; `on_key/on_click/on_blur/on_paste/on_wheel -> (Outcome, Option<FormEvent>)`; plus `FormField::pair(a,b)`. Adopted by: tablepro connections and its filter editor (via FormDialog), showcase forms, settings and selectable.
4. **`MasterDetail`**: `MasterDetail::new(list_id, detail_id).min_split(n).detail_pct(p).collapse_below(w)`; `render(area, ctx, |main|, |detail|)`; `on_key(key, &mut Focus)`; `toggle_drawer()`; `menu_item()`. Adopted by: holla cleanup, disk, files, finder, plan; jackin accounts, manager, usage; tablepro connections, workbench, tabs; showcase app, taskrunner, selectable.
5. **Extensions to `DataTable`/`ListBox`/`TreeView`**: `Column::marker()`, `RowMark{dirty,pointer}`, `ListItem::header()`, `ListItem::status(Tone)`, `TreeNode::{meta_tone, trailing, label_tone}`, `TreeView::columns(Vec<Column>)`. Adopted by: every hand-drawn list, table and tree above.
6. **`HelpOverlay::from_keymaps(sections: &[(&str, &Keymap)])`**: one help surface, generated from the real bindings. Adopted by: all 4 apps.
7. **`FilterTree` / `FilterList`**: `FilterTree{input: TextInput, tree: TreeView}`; `render`; `on_key -> (Outcome, Option<TreeEvent>)`. It owns `/` to edit, Down into the list, Tab, and `set_filter`. Adopted by: tablepro connections and workbench (2 sites), showcase sidebar, jackin's 3 filter prompts.
8. **`EditorShell`**: `EditorShell::new(tabs, cancel, save)`; `on_key(key, focus) -> ShellEvent{Tab(i), Body, Save, Leave}`; `render(area,buf,ctx) -> body Rect`; built-in unsaved-changes prompt and change count. Adopted by: jackin settings and editor; later tablepro's commit flow.
9. **`PageHeader` / `Heading`**: `PageHeader::new(title).status(Vec<Segment>).right(Vec<Segment>).subtitle(s, Tone).render(area,buf,ctx) -> u16`; `Heading::new(t).meta(m).render(...)`. Adopted by: the 11 holla headers, about 17 jackin headings, showcase app.
10. **`NavList`** (moved from showcase sidebars.rs): `NavList::new(id, Vec<NavItem{label,icon,section,badge,disabled}>).current(i).collapsed(b)`. Adopted by: the showcase sidebar, and later any app sidebar.
11. **`TextViewport::find()` + `FindState`**, plus `line_numbers(bool)`: `FindState{query,matches,at}` with `on_key`, `on_paste`, `meta()`, `apply(&mut TextViewport)`. Adopted by: holla activity and files; the jackin cockpit log.
12. **`ActionRow`**: `ActionRow::new(&mut [&mut Button]).message(text, Tone).align(Right)`. Adopted by: holla snapshot, activity, review; tablepro connections and tabs; showcase forms, settings, selectable.
13. **`DetailCard`**: `DetailCard::new(title).meta(m).props(v).actions(..).empty(EmptyState).render(area,buf,ctx) -> Rect`. Adopted by: tablepro connections, history, affected-rows and plan cards; showcase selectable.
14. **`CommandMenu<T,C>`** (moved from jackin rowmenu.rs) plus **`ContextAnchor::row(list, i, area, &scroll)`**: `open(target, anchor, title, Vec<(MenuItem, C)>)`, `on_key/on_click -> (Outcome, Option<(T,C)>)`. Adopted by: jackin accounts, manager, config; the holla mod.rs row-menu helpers.
15. **`FileBrowser`** (moved from jackin modals.rs), with file-system access behind a trait. Adopted by: jackin prelude and config.
16. **`StepLine` + `Wizard<S>`**: `StepLine::new(&[Step])`, the inline form of `StepRail`; `Wizard` handles steps, going back and the Back/Next buttons. `ChoiceDialog::stepper` and `FileBrowser` should take `&[Step]` instead of a string. Adopted by: jackin prelude; showcase taskrunner via StepRail with progress.
17. **Small helpers:**
    - `scrollbar::render_with_fade`
    - `Tabs::layout` / `Tabs::passive`
    - `MeterList(&[(label, pct, value)])`
    - `Notice::error(msg).detail(d).max_lines(n)`
    - `TextInput` right-side readout slot, `render_state`, and `on_key_focus` (replaces the 19 `CommittedTab` → focus next/prev glue sites)
    - `PickerItem.value: usize`
    - `TooSmall`
    - `ui::text::{plural, truncate_sep, duration_ms, bytes, crumb_fit, center_x}`
    - `Theme::centred_hints()` preset

**Build order:**
1. `AppShell` with `Screen`/`Cx`/`ModalKind`. It is the largest saving and fixes the blur drift.
2. The DataTable/ListBox/TreeView extensions, which also remove most of the key-handling and scrollbar copies.
3. `FormPanel`.
4. `MasterDetail`.
5. `HelpOverlay::from_keymaps`.
6. Everything else is quick, low-risk work.
