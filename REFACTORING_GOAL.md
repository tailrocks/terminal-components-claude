# Historical baseline capture contract

## §6 Before-refactor visual evidence

`tools/baseline_capture.sh` names this heading in its source and generated manifest. Its `baseline/before/` default describes historical capture provenance; it does not define current product behavior, prove current parity, or authorize capture execution or baseline updates. Current product authority and acceptance work are described in [product](docs/product.md) and [plan](docs/plan.md). Rendered comparisons must identify their actual source revision separately from a work-index revision.
